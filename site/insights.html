Insights

Legal context for better decisions.

Short general-information notes drawn from the areas in which the firm advises. They are not a substitute for advice on the facts of a specific matter.

Commercial agreements and business decisions

Commercial agreements should state the parties’ responsibilities, key obligations and the framework for managing risk. Legal review can help identify issues that may affect performance, compliance or dispute exposure.

Data protection and organisational accountability

Data privacy is not only a policy document. Organisations should consider how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared and governed in day-to-day operations.

Property transactions and due diligence

Property transactions involve documentation, ownership checks, contractual terms and transfer processes. Reviewing these issues before funds and documents move can help clarify the legal position.

Artificial intelligence and legal governance

Organisations adopting AI should consider governance, privacy, contractual, accountability and regulatory questions alongside the operational benefits of the technology.
