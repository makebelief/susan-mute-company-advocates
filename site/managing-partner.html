Profile

Legal experience across business, regulation and technology.

Susan Mute is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, a member of the Law Society of Kenya, and the Founding & Managing Partner of Susan Mute & Company Advocates.

Her experience spans corporate and commercial law, employment, data protection, property, regulatory compliance and technology-related legal matters, including work with businesses, governments and NGOs.

She has contributed to Media Council of Kenya work on media, AI, data and social-media guidelines, and regularly writes on privacy, data compliance and artificial intelligence regulation.
