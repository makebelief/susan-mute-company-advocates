Website privacy

This website is designed to minimise collection of personal information. The enquiry form prepares an email in the visitor’s own email application rather than submitting information to a website database. Calls and WhatsApp actions open the relevant application on the visitor’s device.

If you contact Susan Mute & Company Advocates directly, information you provide may be used to respond to the enquiry, assess whether the firm can assist and communicate about the matter. Do not send highly sensitive or confidential information through the website before the firm has confirmed the appropriate channel for it.

Third-party services such as Google Maps and WhatsApp may process information under their own privacy terms when you use those services.
